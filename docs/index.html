Overview

Modern audio generation predominantly relies on latent-space compression, which introduces complexity and potential information loss. WavFlow challenges this paradigm by generating high-fidelity audio directly in raw waveform space. By utilizing waveform patchifying and amplitude lifting, WavFlow enables stable flow matching via direct x-prediction, bypassing intermediate representations entirely. Achieving state-of-the-art results on VGGSound and MovieGen-Audio, WavFlow demonstrates that latent compression is not a prerequisite for high-quality synthesis, offering a simpler and more scalable alternative for multimodal generation.
